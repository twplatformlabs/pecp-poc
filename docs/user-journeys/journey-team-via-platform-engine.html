Front-stage actions
  • Opens the platform engine, chooses Create team
  • Names the team; sets the initial owner, admins, and members up front OPEN Q5
  • If approval is required: submits and waits for a decision
  • Otherwise: proceeds immediately
Self-service gate: the RBAC + optional-approval step we scoped in the persona exercise. The platform decides: auto-approve, or route to Platform PO / Org owner.
  • Sees the team created — live in the platform engine and reflected in GitHub
  • Manages the team from the platform engine or GitHub — members, admins
  • Creates repositories for the team
Hands off: creating team repos leads into Flow 5 (repo for team via platform engine).
  • Winds the team down via the platform engine (or GitHub) OPEN Q6
Back-stage actions
  • Platform engine presents the guided team-creation form
  • RBAC check against the alpha entitlement: is the user allowed to create a team? OPEN Q7
  • Policy decides auto-approve vs route for approval + owner assignment OPEN Q1
  • On approval, the create is authorized to proceed
  • Platform engine creates the team record and projects a matching GitHub team (reverse of Flow 3), idempotent wiring
  • Team is not mapped to an Entra group — bypassed for now (only GHE + org access tie to AD)
  • Assigns the owner and applies membership OPEN Q2
  • Changes on either surface reconcile bidirectionally (Platform Engine ↔ GitHub)
  • Records team membership & ownership; audit trail captured
  • Platform engine cleans up the team record; handles team-owned repos OPEN Q6
  • GitHub team removed via the sync
  • (Likely Phase 2) audit / evidence record retained for Compliance

Open questions

Q1
Approval policy. What decides auto-approve vs route-for-approval — team-count limits, naming, domain, something else? Who approves: Platform PO, Org owner, or both by case?
Q2
Owner assignment. Is the creator always the initial owner, or can they name someone else? Can an approver reassign the owner as part of approving?
Q3 ✓
Resolved — Entra backing. Teams are not mapped to Entra for now — deliberately bypassed until needed. Only GitHub Enterprise and org access tie to AD groups.
Q4 ✓
Resolved — source of truth. Both surfaces are valid edit points and sync bidirectionally. The platform engine is the central, up-to-date record across all integrated systems (GitHub now; JFrog, K8s, Datadog later), regardless of where a team was born.
Q5
Team naming. Naming convention for platform-engine-created teams — and does it match the GitHub-created convention (Flow 3 Q1)?
Q6
Team deletion. What happens to team-owned repos on delete — reassign, orphan, or block until moved? Who can delete: owner only, or also Platform PO?
Q7
Creation limits. You noted alpha users can create any number of teams — confirm no cap, and whether that holds once approval routing is on.
